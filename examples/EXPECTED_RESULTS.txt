EasyHap demonstration dataset

Shared inputs:
- sample_group.tsv: 120 samples (40 Cultivar, 40 Landrace, and 40 Wild) with optional latitude/longitude and location labels for geographic haplotype plotting
- traits.tsv: two numeric traits with designed haplotype effects
- demo.gff3: one complete gene model at ChrDemo:1000-2800
- regions.tsv: ChrDemo:900-2900

VCF inputs:
- demo_haploid_multiallelic.vcf: 120 haploid samples
- demo_diploid_multiallelic.vcf: 120 phased diploid samples
- demo_triploid_multiallelic.vcf: 120 phased triploid samples

Suggested command for the new visualization functions:
easyhap analyze --vcf demo_diploid_multiallelic.vcf --region-file regions.tsv --outdir demo_results --group sample_group.tsv --traits traits.tsv --gff demo.gff3 --plot --map-style both

New reproducible outputs include:
- *.HaplotypeMapPie.pdf: geographic haplotype composition shown as pies
- *.HaplotypeMapStackedBar.pdf: geographic haplotype composition shown as stacked bars
- *.HaplotypeNetwork.pdf: minimum-spanning haplotype network
- *.HaplotypeNetworkEdges.tsv: network edges and mutational distances

Gene-feature filtering examples:
- --gene-feature exon retains the demo variants that fall within annotated exons
- --gene-feature cds retains 7 of the 8 demo variants (the position 2700 variant is in the annotated 3' UTR)
- --gene-feature intron finds no demo variants in this particular VCF and the region is therefore skipped when --min-variants >= 1

Optional Fisher filtering settings:
- Fisher comparison: Cultivar versus Landrace
- Fisher alpha: 0.05
- Fisher adjustment: none
- Cluster threshold: 0.20
- Region: ChrDemo:900-2900
